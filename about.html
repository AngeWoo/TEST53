ABOUT US

Bubble Tea Takes Taiwan to the World

珍珠奶茶起源於台灣，如今已風靡全球，從亞洲到歐美，處處可見這款獨特飲品的身影。珍珠奶茶不僅是一種飲料，更是台灣文化的代表，將台灣的美食創意帶向世界各地。

Bubble tea originated in Taiwan and has now swept the globe. From Asia to Europe and America, this unique beverage can be found everywhere. Bubble tea is not just a drink, but a symbol of Taiwanese culture, bringing Taiwan's culinary creativity to every corner of the world.
